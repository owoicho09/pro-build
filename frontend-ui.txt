We are now doing a FRONTEND/UI IMPLEMENTATION PASS for proBuild.

The product architecture and backend behavior already exist and must be preserved.

Your job in this phase is to redesign the frontend so it closely matches the approved visual direction:

- clean modern SaaS application shell
- strong left sidebar
- blue/indigo primary accent
- lots of white space
- visually rich template cards
- strong project thumbnails
- refined cards and inputs
- subtle shadows and borders
- rounded corners
- premium but restrained visual language
- responsive desktop/tablet/mobile behavior
- light/dark theme support

This is not a conceptual redesign exercise.
The visual direction is already decided.

Do not change backend behavior unless required to support the UI.
Do not rewrite working Supabase/v0/Vercel logic.

==================================================
1. OVERALL VISUAL DIRECTION
==================================================

The product should feel like a polished modern AI builder.

Primary characteristics:

- white/light neutral application canvas
- subtle gray borders
- soft card elevation
- rounded corners
- indigo/blue-purple accent
- strong typography hierarchy
- generous spacing
- large visual cards
- minimal clutter
- restrained status badges
- consistent iconography
- strong preview imagery
- polished hover/focus states
- subtle transitions
- no excessive gradients
- no excessive glassmorphism
- no random bright colors
- no generic bootstrap/dashboard look

The interface should feel visually closer to premium products such as modern AI builders and design tools, but remain distinctly proBuild.

Do not copy another product pixel-for-pixel.
